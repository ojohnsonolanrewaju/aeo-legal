Common questions

Can you help if I live outside Nigeria?

Yes. We regularly work with clients in the diaspora by phone, video call and email, and can handle property, estate and corporate matters in Lagos on your behalf.

How do I verify a property before I buy in Lagos?

Before paying, a lawyer should conduct a search at the Lagos State Land Registry, confirm the seller's title documents and check for government acquisition or encumbrances. We can carry out this due diligence for you.

How long does it take to register a company?

Timelines depend on the Corporate Affairs Commission and the type of entity. We will give you a realistic estimate and handle the filings from name reservation to certificate.

Is what I tell you kept confidential?

Yes. Information you share with us in seeking legal advice is treated as confidential in line with our professional obligations.
