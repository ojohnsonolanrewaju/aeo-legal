Privacy Notice

How we handle the personal data you share with us through this website.

This notice explains how AEO Legal Services ("we", "us") handles personal data collected through this website, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

When you contact us or request a consultation, we collect the details you provide: your name, email address, phone number, the area of law and a description of your matter, and any preferred consultation date and time.

Why we use it

We use these details only to respond to your enquiry, arrange a consultation, check for conflicts of interest and, if you instruct us, provide legal services. We rely on your consent and, once you become a client, on our engagement with you and our legal obligations.

How your enquiry reaches us

Messages sent through the contact form are delivered to our email inbox through a form-processing provider, or through your own email app. These providers handle the message under their own privacy terms. This website does not use advertising trackers.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only where necessary to act on your instructions, or where required by law or by the rules of our profession.

Retention

Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted within [period, e.g. 12 months]. Client records are kept for as long as our professional and legal obligations require.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to its use, or withdraw consent at any time by emailing aezekiel@aeolegalservices.com. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
