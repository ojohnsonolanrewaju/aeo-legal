Core practice

Corporate & Commercial

We help entrepreneurs, SMEs and investors put the right legal structure in place and keep it compliant, so the business can focus on growth.

  • Business name and company registration (CAC)
  • Shareholder and partnership agreements
  • Commercial contracts and terms of business
  • Annual returns and company secretarial support
  • Foreign investment and joint ventures
  • Regulatory compliance

Core practice

Family Law

Family matters are personal. We give calm, confidential advice and represent clients firmly where agreement isn't possible.

  • Divorce and judicial separation
  • Child custody, access and maintenance
  • Pre-nuptial and settlement agreements
  • Marriage registration and related documentation
  • Adoption and guardianship
  • Domestic disputes

Core practice

Debt Recovery

We act for individuals and businesses to recover outstanding debts, starting with firm demand and negotiation and moving to court action when needed.

  • Letters of demand
  • Negotiated settlements and payment plans
  • Recovery suits, including undefended list procedure
  • Enforcement of judgments
  • Debt portfolio reviews for SMEs
  • Advice on credit terms to prevent future bad debt

Property & Real Estate

Property is often a client's largest investment. We carry out the checks that protect it and prepare the documents that secure it.

  • Title verification and land registry searches
  • Deeds of assignment, leases and tenancy agreements
  • Governor's consent and perfection of title
  • Advice to developers and off-plan buyers
  • Landlord and tenant disputes
  • Property transactions for clients abroad

Litigation & Dispute Resolution

We prepare every dispute thoroughly and advise honestly on prospects, costs and the quickest sensible route to resolution.

  • Commercial and civil litigation
  • Pre-action negotiation
  • Mediation and arbitration
  • Injunctions and urgent applications
  • Appeals
  • Contract disputes

Wills, Probate & Estates

We help clients plan for their families' future and guide executors and beneficiaries through probate.

  • Drafting and reviewing wills
  • Letters of administration and grant of probate
  • Estate administration and distribution
  • Trusts and succession planning
  • Estate disputes

Employment & Labour

We advise employers and employees on their rights and obligations and help resolve disputes before they escalate.

  • Employment contracts and staff handbooks
  • Disciplinary and termination procedures
  • Workplace disputes
  • Representation at the National Industrial Court
  • HR policy reviews

Intellectual Property

We help businesses and creatives register, protect and license their intellectual property in Nigeria.

  • Trademark searches and registration
  • Copyright protection
  • Licensing and assignment agreements
  • Infringement and passing-off claims

Criminal Defence

Anyone facing a criminal allegation is entitled to representation. We act quickly to protect our clients' rights at every stage.

  • Advice following arrest or invitation by the police
  • Bail applications
  • Representation at trial
  • Fundamental rights enforcement
  • Appeals
