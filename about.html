About the Firm

Built around our clients' confidence

AEO Legal Services is a Lagos law firm established in 2026, serving individuals, SMEs and Nigerians in the diaspora.

Our Story

Why we started AEO

We established AEO Legal Services to meet our clients' needs proactively, to resolve their issues promptly, and to give them confidence at every stage of their journey.

Too often, people come to a lawyer late, when a dispute has already escalated or a deal is already at risk. We want to be the firm clients call early: one that explains their position plainly, acts without delay and keeps them informed.

From our office in Lagos, and remotely for clients across Nigeria and abroad, we advise on corporate and commercial matters, family law, debt recovery, property, disputes and more.
