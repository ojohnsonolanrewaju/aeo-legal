FAQs

Common questions

Quick answers to the questions clients ask us most. For advice on your situation, book a consultation.

Consultations

Do you charge for consultations?

Yes. Consultations are paid. The fee depends on the nature of the matter and is confirmed when you book, before the consultation takes place.

Can I consult you remotely?

Yes. In addition to meetings at our office, we offer consultations by phone and video call. Remote consultations are available outside office hours by appointment.

I live outside Nigeria. Can you help?

Yes. We regularly assist Nigerians in the diaspora with property, family, estate and business matters in Nigeria, communicating by email, phone and video call.

What should I bring to a consultation?

Any documents related to your matter, such as contracts, title documents, correspondence, court papers or receipts, and a short timeline of events. If in doubt, bring it.

Business

How do I register a company in Nigeria?

Companies are registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC). The process includes name reservation, preparing incorporation documents and filing. We handle the full process and advise on the right structure for your business.

Someone owes my business money. What can I do?

The first step is usually a formal letter of demand from a lawyer, which often leads to payment or a settlement. If not, we can file a recovery action in court and, after judgment, take steps to enforce it.

Family & Property

How does divorce work in Nigeria?

For statutory marriages, divorce is by petition to the High Court on the ground that the marriage has broken down irretrievably, shown by one of the facts set out in the Matrimonial Causes Act. We advise on the process, custody, maintenance and property before you take any step.

How do I check a property before buying in Lagos?

Before paying, a lawyer should search the title at the land registry, confirm the seller's documents and check for government acquisition, charges or disputes. We carry out this due diligence and prepare the transfer documents.

Working with us

Is what I tell you confidential?

Yes. Information you share with us in seeking legal advice is treated as confidential in line with our professional obligations.

Do I become a client by contacting you?

No. A lawyer–client relationship begins once we have agreed in writing to act for you. Until then, please don't send highly sensitive documents through the website.
